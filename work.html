WHAT I SHIPPED

During this internship I audited internal and client-facing APIs with a strong focus on authorization and business-logic testing. Built a repeatable methodology for API security reviews and automated parts of it with custom scripts.

Authorization testing: Enumerated object IDs, tested IDOR and BOLA across multi-tenant endpoints, validated horizontal/vertical privilege escalation.

JWT & session: Tested none-alg, weak secret, kid confusion, jku/x5u, token replay, and expiration bypasses.

Tooling: Built ZAP automation (auth scripts, active scan policies), Burp match/replace flows, and Postman + Newman collections for regression.

Reporting: Wrote proof-of-concepts with impact analysis and remediation PRs — not just CVSS scores.
